My recommendation

I wouldn't jump straight to more UI polish yet. The strongest next step is v3.3 Repository Intelligence.

Once Buster has a complete understanding of its own codebase—call graphs, dependencies, symbol search, and relationships—the Planner and Agent Team become much more capable because they can reason about the entire project instead of individual files. That foundation will also make later Vision, Voice, and automation features easier to integrate cleanly.
